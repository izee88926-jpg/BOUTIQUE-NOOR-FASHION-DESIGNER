## Brand & Style

This design system defines the digital flagship for a luxury Pakistani bridal, formal, and pret atelier. The visual language blends the heritage of South Asian craftsmanship with the restraint of European editorial fashion publishing.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Aristocratic Restraint:** Dignified, architectural, and serene. It shuns the clutter often found in commercial e-commerce in favor of high-fashion portfolio pacing.
- **Craftsmanship & Reverence:** Micro-details evoke zardozi embroidery, raw silk weaves, and hand-beaded borders through razor-sharp lines and antique gold accents.
- **Intimacy & Bespoke Service:** Creates the intimacy of a private salon appointment through discreet bespoke tailoring inquiries, multi-currency clarity (with Pakistani Rupee Rs. as standard), and bespoke concierge actions (direct atelier WhatsApp consultations).

### Design Movement
- **Editorial High Fashion & Modern Heritage:** Dominated by expansive warm ivory canvases, severe typographic scale contrast, delicate hairline gold borders, and architectural framing inspired by subcontinental cusped arches and jaali screens.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from traditional royal subcontinental silks, antique metallic bullion, and untreated natural parchment.

- **Primary (`#0F4D3A` - Deep Emerald Green):** The sovereign shade of the atelier. Reserved for statement interactive states, key editorial badges, primary headers, and signature collection anchors.
- **Secondary (`#C9A24B` - Antique Metallic Brass/Gold):** Evokes antique beaten gold threadwork. Used for secondary CTAs, key accent dividers, bespoke availability tags, and focused state rings.
- **Tertiary (`#092E23` - Rich Forest Black):** Grounding contrast for high-density footer panels, dark luxury lookbook takeovers, and inverted modal frames.
- **Neutral (`#2B2B2B` - Deep Charcoal):** The primary reading ink. Replaces harsh true black to preserve typographic warmth across editorial copy on light surfaces.
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF6F0` - Warm Parchment Ivory):** The foundational backdrop. Offers an organic, museum-like warmth that makes silk, velvet, and organza photography radiate without clinical glare.
- **Structural Border (`#E7DEC8` - Muted Warm Gold-Mist):** Subtle borders for product grids, architectural dividers, and framing elements.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic couture grandeur and modern utility.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Rendered in **Playfair Display**, using delicate transitional serifs, high stroke contrast, and natural italic inflections for sub-collections (e.g., *The Shehnai Edit*, *Zardozi Atelier*).
- **Body & Functional Narrative:** Built on **Plus Jakarta Sans** at light and regular weights. Its geometric clarity balances the expressive weight of the serif display type.
- **Labels, Navigation & Metadata:** Rendered in strict uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.12em` to `0.18em`) using Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Price Format:** Always styled consistently as `Rs. 185,000` or `PKR 185,000`. Currency symbols are displayed in medium-weight sans-serif to ensure legibility across catalog grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around asymmetric editorial grids, generous white space, and art-directed portrait framing for garments.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop leverages a 12-column layout with 2.5rem gutters. Product listings honor a 2-column or 3-column asymmetric cadence (allowing high-resolution full-length bridal views). Mobile adapts to an edge-to-edge single-column or 2-column staggered scroll.
- **Margin Hierarchy:** Expansive outer borders (5rem on desktop viewports) frame the content like a gallery monograph. Mobile settles into a strict 1.5rem margin.
- **Negative Space Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-2xl`) separate bridal silhouettes, artisan notes, and textile provenance modules, avoiding commercial crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on drop shadows. It uses surface contrast, hairline metallic outlines, and soft backdrop diffusion.

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation shifts from the foundation canvas (`#FAF6F0`) to elevated cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed in hairline `#E7DEC8`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Components sit flat with 1px precise structural borders (`#E7DEC8`). When active or focused, these borders warm into `#C9A24B`.
- **Ambient Diffusion:** Overlays, size recommendation drawers, and couture inquiry modals use a semi-sheer parchment blur (`rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) rather than dark dimmers.
- **Hover Micro-Depth:** Interactive garments gently shift tone or zoom subtly (1.02x image scaling over 500ms cubic bezier transitions) rather than casting aggressive box shadows.

## Shapes

The design system uses architectural geometry, reflecting South Asian heritage through razor-sharp lines and curated motifs.

- **Corner Radius:** Zero radius (`roundedness: 0`). Buttons, product tiles, input fields, modals, and badge tags feature crisp, right-angled corners.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Arch-shaped masks and subtle cusped frames are applied to key editorial hero portraits and bespoke campaign lookbooks. These architectural accents complement the sharp framing of the interface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Couture CTA:** Solid `#0F4D3A` fill, crisp rectangular edges, `#FAF6F0` text, label-caps typography (`font-size: 11px`, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`). On hover, transitions to `#092E23` with a 1px inner border in `#C9A24B`.
- **Secondary Atelier Action:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#0F4D3A` or `#C9A24B`, text colored to match the border. Hover triggers an ivory wash with solid gold line definition.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Dedicated couture styling action. Outlined in antique gold `#C9A24B` with emerald text `#0F4D3A` and a WhatsApp monogram icon. Used for custom bridal orders, made-to-measure sizing, and private showroom bookings.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean vertical orientation supporting 4:5 or 2:3 aspect ratio portrait photography.
- **Framing:** Optional 1px border in `#E7DEC8`. No elevation shadows.
- **Details Section:** Placed below the image with generous internal top-padding (`space-md`). Features the title in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm` or `title-editorial`), fabric tag (e.g., *Pure Raw Silk 80g / Hand-worked Zardozi*), and price in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction:** Image cross-fades smoothly to back craftsmanship or unstitched fabric close-ups. Quick actions ("Order Bespoke", "Request Swatch") reveal on desktop hover via a bottom-aligned bar.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Underlined border only (`border-bottom: 1px solid #E7DEC8`), zero side borders, transparent background on `#FAF6F0`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#C9A24B` with an elevated label shift.
- **Measurement Selectors:** Segmented grid selectors for bespoke measurements (Kameez Length, Chest, Waist, Shoulder, Gharara Flare) with clear millimeter/inch toggles and zero-radius active chips.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** "Bridal Couture", "Ready to Ship", "Handcrafted Pret", and "Made to Order".
- **Styling:** Minimalist uppercase text enclosed in a 1px `#E7DEC8` border, padded by `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally. Background remains transparent or soft warm cream `#F3EDE2`.

### Lists & Atelier Accordions
- **Craftsmanship Accordions:** Used for "Fabric & Care", "Bespoke Delivery Timeline (6–8 Weeks)", and "Artisan Heritage". Separated by 1px solid `#E7DEC8` dividers.
- **Indicator:** Minimalist `+` and `−` symbols rendered in `#C9A24B`.
