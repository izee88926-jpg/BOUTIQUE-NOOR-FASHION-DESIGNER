Concierge Inquiries

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

How far in advance should I book my bridal commission?

expand_more

We recommend initiating your couture design 3 to 6 months prior to your wedding festivities. This permits ample time for swatch approvals, muslin toile adjustments, 500+ hours of hand-embroidery, and DHL Express doorstep delivery anywhere worldwide. Rush orders within 8 weeks are accommodated on special executive approval.

How do virtual appointments work for international brides?

expand_more

Over 60% of Noor brides reside in the UK, USA, Canada, and the GCC. Our virtual appointments utilize high-definition multi-angle cameras to display fabric sheen, true metallic thread colors, and fluid drape. We also provide our proprietary 18-point measurement guide and mail custom tactile fabric swatches directly to your residence.

What is the payment structure for custom heirloom pieces?

expand_more

All bespoke orders proceed with a 50% initial commission retainer upon finalization of sketches. 25% is settled upon completion of the toile fitting stage, and the final 25% balance is completed prior to final dispatch via fully insured international express freight or upon atelier salon pickup.
